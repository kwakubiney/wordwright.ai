Wordwright + Jev: input/output contract

flowchart TD
    A[Wordwright Settings] --> S1[(chrome.storage.local)]
    S1 --> C[AI configuration]
    C --> C1[TypeSafe API key]
    U[Learner] --> Q[Assessment UI]
    Q --> Q1[Target word]
    Q --> Q2[Definition]
    Q --> Q3[Exercise mode]
    Q --> Q4[Learner sentence]
    Q4 --> E[evaluateSentence]
    Q1 --> E
    Q2 --> E
    Q3 --> E
    E --> JREQ
    subgraph JREQ[Request to Jev]
      JR1[POST /v1/systemone]
      JR2[Bearer TypeSafe key]
      JR3[state: word, definition, sentence, context]
      JR4[questions: score + 3 nouls]
    end
    JREQ --> JE[Jev System One]
    subgraph JE[Jev]
      JE1[Typed decision model]
      JE2[Probabilities]
      JE3[Confidence]
      JE4[No prose generation]
    end
    JE --> JRES
    subgraph JRES[Jev response]
      JR5[usage_quality: score 0-4]
      JR6[meaning_correct: noul 0-1]
      JR7[grammar_correct: noul 0-1]
      JR8[natural_usage: noul 0-1]
    end
    JRES --> V[Wordwright validation]
    V -->|Malformed or uncertain| F[Self-grade dialog]
    V -->|Valid and confident| D[Deterministic mapping]
    D --> D1[App score: round raw score + 1]
    D --> D2[Category: meaning, grammar, naturalness]
    D --> P[Authoritative judgment]
    P --> TXT[Deterministic teaching feedback]
    TXT --> UI[Feedback UI]
    P --> SR[SRS processing]
    SR --> H[Review history / next review]
